Inadequate Garbage Disposal and Cleanliness

📍 Locations: Aliganj, Ashoka Hotel Sector, Adarsh Nagar 📮 Pincodes: 110003, 110021, 110033 📋 Threshold: 10 Citizen Upvotes Status: Escalated to Municipality

Overflowing public dumpsters and irregular municipal waste collection have created hygiene hazards and foul odors on residential lanes. Stray animals and uncollected organic waste block storm gutters.

Prompt installation of secondary covered collection bins, regularized two-shift pickup cycles, and automated compactor trucks are urgently needed to maintain community health and sanitation standards.
